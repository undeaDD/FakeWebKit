# Code of Conduct

FakeWebKit is a small open source library. We want everyone who takes part in this
project (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) to be treated with respect.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, patient and welcoming, especially towards people new to the project or to Swift
- Respecting different opinions, experiences and use cases
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the people using the library and the apps built with it

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (such as a postal or email address) without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct,
and will explain the reason when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions)
and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour can be reported to the maintainer by
[email](mailto:dominic.drees@live.de?subject=FakeWebKit%20Code%20of%20Conduct).
All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.
Security vulnerabilities are not handled here: please follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Depending on the severity, the maintainer may respond with:

1. **Correction:** a private note explaining why the behaviour was inappropriate, possibly with a request for a public apology
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction with the people involved for a while
3. **Temporary ban:** no interaction with the project for a set period of time
4. **Permanent ban:** no further participation in the project
